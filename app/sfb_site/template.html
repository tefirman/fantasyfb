05 — Method

How these odds are made

01

Every league, every week

Each Sleeper league is simulated through week __REG_WEEKS__ with the weekly median game, using fantasyfb's player projections. Completed weeks use real scores.

02

Shared player draws

Each NFL player gets one random performance per week and simulation, shared by every team starting him, so big games lift the whole field together instead of canceling out across leagues.

03

Qualifying

Teams with __AUTO_WINS__ or more wins qualify; the rest of the field fills by points, scaled to the teams simulated (__QUOTA__ of __TEAMS__). Wins and points are simulation averages, so teams near a line can go either way.

04

Playoff rounds

In weeks 12-17 a team's round score is its points-per-game average (times 1 in weeks 12-14, times 0.5 in weeks 15-17) plus that week's score. The field shrinks to __ROUND_SIZES__ teams, and the top week 17 score wins.

05

Player swing

Reads the shared draws back as an experiment, for a one-standard-deviation better stretch. Qualification swing covers the remaining regular-season weeks; playoff swing covers weeks 12-17. It reflects projected mean and spread, not scouting, and only players started on at least 50 teams are shown.

06

Limits

The 17 MyFantasyLeague leagues are not simulated. Projections reflect rosters, injuries, and lineups when the data was collected, so they will move. Seeding and tiebreakers are not described in the SFB rules, so ties are not specially handled. These are estimates, not official standings.
