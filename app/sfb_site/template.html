    How these odds are made
    • Every Sleeper league in the field is simulated through week 11, with the weekly median game, using fantasyfb's player projections. Completed weeks use real scores.
    • Each NFL player gets one random performance per week and simulation that every team starting him shares, so big games lift the whole field together instead of canceling out across leagues.
    • Teams with 13 or more wins qualify, and the rest of the field fills by points, scaled to the teams simulated (__QUOTA__ of __TEAMS__).
    • In weeks 12-17 a team's round score is its points-per-game average over every game played so far (times 1 in weeks 12-14, times 0.5 in weeks 15-17) plus that week's score. The field shrinks to __ROUND_SIZES__ teams, and the top week 17 score wins. These counts are scaled to the teams simulated.
    • Each dot under "Where every team stands" is one team. The dashed red lines mark 13 wins, which qualifies a team automatically, and the points cut line, with the shaded band covering the 5th to 95th percentile of that line across simulations. Wins and points are simulation averages, so teams near a line can go either way.
    • A player's swing reads the shared draws back as an experiment: in the simulations where he has a better stretch, how much more often do the teams starting him do well? It is measured for a one-standard-deviation better stretch. Qualification swing covers the remaining regular-season weeks and is in percentage points of qualification odds; playoff swing covers weeks 12-17 and is in playoff rounds advanced, for teams that made it. It reflects each player's projected mean and spread, not scouting, and only players started on at least 50 teams are shown.
    • The 17 MyFantasyLeague leagues are not simulated. Projections reflect rosters, injuries, and lineups when the data was collected, so they will move.
    • Seeding and tiebreakers are not described in the SFB rules, so exact ties are not specially handled. These are estimates, not official standings.
